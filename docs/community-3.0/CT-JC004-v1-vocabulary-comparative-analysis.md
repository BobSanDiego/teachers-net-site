# CT-JC004 — V1 Vocabulary Comparative Analysis

Status: analysis and Engineering Director decision matrix  
Date: 2026-07-30  
Evidence boundary: CT-JC001 and CT-JC003 only

## 1. Executive findings

The existing Grade Level tree is broad enough to represent the observed external grade vocabulary without a V1 change. External sources use different legal, school-division, and marketplace boundaries, but they repeatedly cover early childhood, elementary, middle, secondary/high school, post-secondary, adult, and all-grade contexts already represented by the current tree.

The existing Subject Area tree covers the principal recurring instructional categories in CT-JC003 and the current Teachers.Net instructional corpus. No current Subject Area term requires retirement or renaming for V1. Current nested terms such as Algebra, Biology, History, Spanish, and Computer Science are compatible with recurring external labels and should remain as child-term evidence without expanding the V1 surface into a deep discipline taxonomy.

The external evidence identifies broad categories absent from the current tree: Dance, Theater/Drama, Business Education, Agriculture, Family and Consumer Science/Home Economics, and Religion/Religious Studies. These are candidate additions for Engineering Director decision, not approved terms. Counseling, psychology, administration, operations, leadership, therapy, student services, and similar support roles are excluded from Subject Area recommendations and recorded as future-axis observations only.

## 5. Candidate exclusions and future-axis observations

Administration, counseling, psychology, therapy, operations, leadership, student services, wellbeing, and other non-curricular support identities are not Subject Area recommendations. Their recurrence supports future role/function/position dimensions, not forced Subject Area assignment. No future-axis design is proposed here.
