# Community Feed Balance Study v1

## Finding

Rich media increases visual salience but is not equivalent to relevance,
quality, trust, or community value. A feed that rewards image dimensions,
preview presence, or attachment count will systematically disadvantage
text-only questions, explanations, announcements, and replies.

## Required evaluation dimensions

Before feed implementation, compare text-only and rich posts across exposure,
open rate, dwell/read completion, reply rate, meaningful interaction,
moderation rate, accessibility completion, and user-controlled preference.
Segment by subject, author experience, device, connection quality, and whether
enrichment succeeded. Do not use engagement alone as a quality proxy.

## Recommendation

Use a balanced card grammar and ranking policy: text receives full-width,
readable treatment; media is a supporting signal; failed previews collapse
cleanly; accessibility and moderation states override presentation. Treat the
study and final ranking policy as required before feed implementation. Any
engagement optimization beyond those controls is speculative.
