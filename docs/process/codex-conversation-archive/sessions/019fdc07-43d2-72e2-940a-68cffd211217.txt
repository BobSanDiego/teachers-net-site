- **Work mode:** best when you want ChatGPT to turn a goal plus files/context into a finished artifact—such as a document, spreadsheet, presentation, analysis, or synthesized report. Official OpenAI guidance describes Work mode this way. ([OpenAI Work mode overview](https://learn.chatgpt.com/))

- Use **Chat** to discuss ticket design, review reports, clarify scope, or decide what should happen next.

The disadvantages of Work mode are mainly practical:

- It can be excessive for a simple question.
- It may spend more time organizing, inspecting files, and producing a finished artifact than you need.
- It can encourage premature execution when you only wanted discussion, so state “read-only” or “do not edit” explicitly when appropriate.
- Because it may perform more work and use more tools/context, it may consume more usage than a short Chat exchange. OpenAI’s public overview does not provide a fixed per-mode quota formula, so the exact impact depends on your plan and the task.

For the document you just created, **Work mode was the better choice**. For your follow-up questions about that document, **Chat mode is the better choice**.
