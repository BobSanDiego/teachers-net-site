# CT-JC005 — Candidate Subject Market Prevalence Audit

Status: bounded research and analysis  
Date: 2026-07-30  
Evidence boundary: CT-JC003, CT-JC004, CT-JC004R, and one additional employer-hiring survey

## 1. Method and limits

This audit evaluates only the five candidates named by the ticket: Dance, Theater / Drama, Business Education, Agriculture, and Family & Consumer Science / Home Economics. It distinguishes employer hiring evidence from certification or taxonomy enumeration. The additional survey used representative employer-facing Tes listings and the ISS international recruitment pages; it was not an exhaustive count of all jobs. “Observed sources” means independent hiring platforms or employer listings, not the number of individual jobs on one platform.

The allowed assessments are used exactly as requested. They are evidence classifications, not taxonomy approvals.

## 3. Candidate-by-candidate evidence notes

### Business Education

The additional employer survey found repeated Tes postings across a wide set of English regions and institution types. The listings include Business Studies Teacher roles at The John of Gaunt School in Wiltshire, Hinchley Wood School in Surrey, Tring Park School in Hertfordshire, University of Birmingham School, Thomas Telford School, St James’ School, and other secondary or sixth-form employers. The listings describe Business Studies as a taught subject, sometimes alongside Economics or vocational courses. This is employer hiring evidence, distinct from the CTC/NYSED/TEA certification evidence.

### Dance

Tes listings included Dance Teacher at ArtsEd in Chiswick, Teacher of Dance at Bishop Wand in Surrey, Teacher of Dance/Drama at Colton Hills in Wolverhampton, and a Teacher of Drama and Dance at Clifton College in Bristol. The postings show Dance as a real employer hiring label, but often within performing arts or in a combined assignment. ISS also exposes Performing Arts as an international recruitment category. The evidence supports common specialty status, not a conclusion that a separate V1 term must be created.

### Theater / Drama

Tes listings included Teacher of Drama at Channing School in Highgate, Drama Teacher at Slough and Eton, Teacher of Drama & Music at Canon Slade School in Bolton, Drama Teacher in Bath and North East Somerset, and combined Drama/Dance roles. The postings demonstrate recurring employer use and geographic breadth, especially in UK secondary and independent schools. The evidence supports common specialty status while leaving the candidate label and any combination with Dance unresolved.

### Agriculture

CT-JC003 recorded Agriculture in California, New York, and Texas authorization frameworks and SchoolSpring’s classroom/career categories. The additional employer-facing survey did not locate a comparable set of independent employer listings for Agriculture within its bounded search. The resulting assessment reflects insufficient demonstrated market breadth in this survey, not a claim that agriculture teaching jobs do not exist.

### Family & Consumer Science / Home Economics

CT-JC003 recorded Home Economics or Family and Consumer Science in California and SchoolSpring evidence. The additional employer-facing survey did not locate a comparable set of independent employer listings under those labels. The assessment therefore remains regional/niche on the collected evidence and does not resolve whether alternate labels such as Consumer Science or Food Technology should be considered in a later study.

## 4. Top-level versus deeper-filter exposure

The collected evidence shows different exposure patterns:

- Business Education is exposed as a direct teacher subject in employer titles and as a marketplace/category concept.
- Dance and Theater/Drama are exposed as direct employer titles, but commonly grouped under Performing Arts or paired with another subject.
- Agriculture and Family/Consumer Science appear more clearly in credential or deep marketplace enumerations than in the bounded independent employer listings.

These observations are not a proposed UI or taxonomy design.

## 5. Ranked evidence strength

1. **Business Education — Core V1 candidate:** strongest cross-source and cross-region employer hiring recurrence in this bounded survey.
2. **Theater / Drama — Common specialty:** repeated independent employer postings and broad UK evidence, with frequent performing-arts grouping.
3. **Dance — Common specialty:** repeated independent employer postings and international/category evidence, with frequent performing-arts grouping.
4. **Agriculture — Regional / niche specialty:** repeated certification and filter enumeration but no comparable independent employer-listing recurrence located in this survey.
5. **Family & Consumer Science / Home Economics — Regional / niche specialty:** limited collected evidence concentrated in certification and marketplace enumeration.

The ranking is a research result under the stated evidence boundary, not approval to add or alter any Core Terms.
