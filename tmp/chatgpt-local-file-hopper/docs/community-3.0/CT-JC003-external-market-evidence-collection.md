# CT-JC003 — External Grade-Level and Subject-Area Market Evidence

Status: data collection only  
Collected: 2026-07-30  
Boundary: no Teachers.Net recommendation, approval, hierarchy change, or implementation decision

## 1. Evidence-set design

This evidence set preserves vocabulary observed in four different contexts:

1. official state educator authorization and preparation frameworks;
2. an education-job marketplace’s employer-facing filters;
3. private-school hiring categories and live titles;
4. international-school recruitment categories and live titles.

These sources are evidence of vocabulary in use. They are not equivalent authorities and none is treated as an automatic Teachers.Net term decision.

## 4. Observations preserved without analysis

- State certification structures describe authorization and preparation, not necessarily the simplest employer posting interface.
- Marketplace filters can be broader and more granular than a V1 product vocabulary.
- Private and international listings expose combinations such as Spanish plus Social Studies, 5th Grade Language Arts, middle/upper-school Spanish, elementary general studies, and subject specialists.
- Similar labels recur with different boundaries: Mathematics/Math, English/Language Arts/ELA, Social Studies/Social Science/Humanities, and World Languages/Foreign Languages.
- Several observed labels may describe a role, credential, pedagogy, program, curriculum, or subject. This collection does not resolve those dimensions.
- The sources also expose non-curricular families such as counseling, psychology, librarian, administration, wellbeing, and support positions. They are recorded as context, not as Subject Area requirements.

## 5. Collection limitations

- State frameworks are legal or preparation evidence, not product-taxonomy specifications.
- SchoolSpring exposes a deep marketplace taxonomy that may be intentionally broader than Teachers.Net V1.
- NAIS and international listings were sampled, not exhaustively counted.
- Search-result availability varies by site and date.
- Labels can overlap role, credential, pedagogy, program, and curriculum dimensions.
- No frequency-weighted recommendation was made.
- No Teachers.Net term is marked for addition, retention, renaming, restructuring, or retirement.
