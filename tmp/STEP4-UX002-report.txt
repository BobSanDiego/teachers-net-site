Root cause/correction
=====================
Step 4 had not yet applied the final progressive-disclosure contract: its
dependent method fields were initialized together with an unset deadline mode,
and contact selection remained radio-based. The implementation now initializes
the method selector with all dependent fields hidden, defaults Deadline Mode to
Open until filled, and uses one Contact Information dropdown.

Implemented behavior
====================
- URL, Email, and Instructions fields reveal exclusively by method.
- Method changes focus the newly revealed field.
- Open until filled hides date controls and appears in preview by default.
- Specific date reveals and focuses the date field and requires a future date.
- Default contact displays the existing LAUSD contact fixture values and the
  Do not publish contact information checkbox.
- Override contact reveals optional prefilled fields and hides the privacy
  checkbox without erasing current-session values.
- Preview synchronizes method, deadline, default/override contact, and privacy
  state immediately.
