Conclusion
==========
The apparent spacing regression is a render-path mismatch, not a timing, cache,
font-loading, or fractional-layout artifact. The Initial view uses the static
legacy `.panel-heading` markup in index.html. Add School and Steps 2-4 use the
newer `.wizard-stage-heading` markup produced by the shared JavaScript renderer.
Those paths have different DOM ownership and different winning CSS rules.

Initial path (live DOM)
-----------------------
`.panel-heading > div > .eyebrow, h2, p` with a panel-heading border and
horizontal padding. The broad legacy rules in mockup.css line 21 win:

- `.eyebrow`: 11px, line-height 15.95px, margin-bottom 10px, letter-spacing
  .22px at this runtime.
- `.panel h2`: 22px with the browser-derived 31.9px line-height.
- `.panel-heading p:not(.eyebrow)`: margin 5px 0 0.
- The support paragraph is nested under the inner div, not a direct heading
  child, and the heading carries a bottom border/padding treatment.

Add School and Steps 2-4 path (live DOM)
----------------------------------------
`.wizard-stage-heading > .eyebrow, h2, p`, created by JavaScript. The canonical
stage rules in mockup.css line 691 win:

- `.eyebrow`: 12px, line-height 17.4px, margin-bottom 4px, letter-spacing
  .24px.
- `.wizard-stage-heading h2`: 22px, line-height 26.4px, margin-bottom 4px.
- `.wizard-stage-heading > p:last-child`: margin 0.
- The heading has margin-bottom 28px and no border/padding of its own.

==============================
Hash transitions were checked at setTimeout(0), first requestAnimationFrame,
second requestAnimationFrame, 250ms, and after document.fonts.ready. Initial
and Add School measurements were stable at every checkpoint; font status was

not synthetic fixtures. The mismatch is therefore present in the shipped
markup/style ownership before and after settling.
