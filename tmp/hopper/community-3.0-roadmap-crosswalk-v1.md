# Community 3.0 Roadmap Crosswalk v1

| Capability family | Reconciled position | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Governance and identity | Preserve WordPress identity and the path/group distinction. | Foundational |
| Visibility and safety | Make visibility, anonymous posting, moderation, retention, and suppression explicit before feature work. | Accelerate |
| Groups and memberships | Reconcile mapping, join/leave, frequency, directories, and administrative actions as separate contracts. | Accelerate mapping; sequence the rest |
| Posts and engagement | Preserve rich posts, replies, reactions, notices, and post-view concepts, but gate delivery and analytics on policy. | Preserve with gates |
| Subscriber policies | Define consent, interests, onboarding, pause, unsubscribe, history, and frequency behavior before mail automation. | Accelerate |
| Notifications and communications | Separate bell, reply notices, transactional mail, digests, newsletters, SES, analytics, and Postfix operations. | Accelerate contracts; defer provider rollout |
| Core Terms and Portable Views | Treat semantic terms and reusable views as shared platform contracts. | Accelerate |
| Relationships and discovery | Require explicit relationship governance, approvals, explainability, and privacy boundaries. | Defer until trust contracts |
| AI and integrations | Keep provider, campaign, AI, and external integration work behind stable contracts. | Defer |

The crosswalk accelerates trust and identity controls, not visible feature volume. It preserves the original Community concepts while making evidence and authority prerequisites explicit.
