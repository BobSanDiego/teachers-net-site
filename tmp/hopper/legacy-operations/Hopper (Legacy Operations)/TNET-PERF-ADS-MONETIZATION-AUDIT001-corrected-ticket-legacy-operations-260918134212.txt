TICKET READY FOR CODEX
Ticket: TNET-PERF-ADS-MONETIZATION-AUDIT001

Mode: DIAGNOSTIC
Reasoning: HIGH
Recommended model: LUNA
Project: legacy-operations
OWNER: Teachers.Net Legacy Operations

OUTCOME

Audit Teachers.Net's live monetization plumbing across representative page
families and determine whether the current site is consistently presenting
eligible AdSense inventory as intended.

This is a diagnostic/audit objective first. Do not optimize ad density or make
production monetization changes without Director review.

BUSINESS EVIDENCE

Recent AdSense comparison shows a systemic monetization-efficiency collapse
versus the same-length 2023 period despite substantial current page volume.

Examples observed by Director:

Site:
2023 Page RPM ~ $1.37
2026 Page RPM ~ $0.09

/mentors:
2023 ~85.9K page views / 11.7K impressions / $17.71
2026 ~101.1K page views / 1.4K impressions / $1.04

/lessons:
2023 ~37.3K page views / 21.9K impressions / $63.22
2026 ~26.1K page views / 5.6K impressions / $9.78

/lessonplans:
2023 ~30.8K page views / 27.3K impressions / $131.45
2026 ~7.1K page views / 2.5K impressions / $7.54

/jobs currently retains comparatively strong RPM.

Treat these as investigation cues, not proof of implementation failure.

AUTHORITY

Inspect current repo/runtime/live page owners first.
Do not infer ad ownership from historical assumptions.

Representative live families must include at minimum:

- homepage/root and legacy index
- main /chat/
- representative /mentors/ board/list/topic routes
- /lessons/
- /lessonplans/ plus representative grade/content route
- /jobs/
- /states/
- /gazette/
- any materially different shared shell/template owner discovered

DIAGNOSTIC QUESTIONS

For each representative family determine:

1. Is the expected AdSense loader/tag present?
2. Are expected ad-slot elements actually emitted?
3. Are slot IDs/configuration consistent and valid?
4. Does runtime JS request/fill the slot?
5. Is the slot hidden, collapsed, offscreen, zero-sized, overlapped, or removed?
6. Do responsive breakpoints suppress inventory?
7. Do consent/CMP/privacy conditions prevent requests?
8. Do CSP, JS errors, race conditions, lazy-load logic, duplicate loaders, or
   template differences prevent initialization?
9. Are legacy templates materially different from newer/shared-shell routes?
10. Are ads.txt / publisher ID / relevant account identifiers consistent?
11. Is large page-volume inventory simply bot/non-monetizable traffic rather
    than missing ad implementation?
12. Is there evidence that old takeover-ad removal also removed or disrupted
    unrelated legitimate placements?

DECISIVE EVIDENCE

Build a route-family ledger showing for every inspected route:

- template/owner
- loader present/absent
- declared slots
- rendered slots
- network ad requests observed
- slot dimensions/visibility
- fill/no-fill where observable
- console/runtime errors
- responsive behavior
- consent state
- implementation anomaly if any

Use native/browser evidence on live pages where possible.
Static source inspection alone is insufficient for runtime conclusions.

Do not treat synthetic DOM injection as acceptance.

ANALYTICS BOUNDARY

Do not attempt to log into or modify Google AdSense/GA4 accounts unless the
Director separately authorizes a specific read-only browser workflow.

If Director-provided exports/screenshots/CSV are available, correlate them with
route-family findings, but keep analytics evidence distinct from runtime proof.

Do not infer revenue causality from implementation evidence alone.

OUTPUT

Return:

1. concise executive finding;
2. monetization coverage ledger by page family;
3. confirmed defects versus non-defect explanations;
4. likely systemic owner(s), if proven;
5. estimated scope of any correction;
6. recommended next objective(s), ranked by evidence strength but without making
   unapproved production changes;
7. any analytics questions that require Director/ChatGPT investigation.

ACCEPTANCE

Audit is complete when:

- all representative page families are covered;
- at least one native/runtime evidence seam exists per materially different
  template owner;
- static and runtime findings are reconciled;
- no unsupported claim is made that revenue decline is caused by missing tags;
- any confirmed implementation defect names one concrete owner;
- unrelated performance work remains intact.

EXCLUSIONS

Do not add more ads.
Do not restore takeover ads.
Do not alter ad density or placement strategy.
Do not change AdSense account settings.
Do not change consent policy.
Do not modify production.
Do not redesign pages.
Do not broaden into SEO or content strategy.

STOP BOUNDARY

Stop after evidence-based diagnosis and recommendation packaging.

If a production fix is obvious and low-risk, identify it but wait for Director
approval before implementation.
