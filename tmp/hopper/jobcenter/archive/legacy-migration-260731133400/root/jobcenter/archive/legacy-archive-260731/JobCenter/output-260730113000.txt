Cause and correction
--------------------
The winning transition came from the existing authority media block at the
stylesheet’s responsive refinement section. Its selector was:

@media (min-width:768px) and (max-width:1199px)

Within that block, the winning declarations changed the left rail to
padding: 22px 14px and the main workspace to padding: 24px 22px 28px.
Those declarations began at 1199px even though the governing architecture
requires no structural desktop transition there.

The block’s upper bound was narrowed to 1024px. The 1024px compact transition
is therefore preserved, while 1201, 1200, 1199, and 1198 retain the same
desktop rail/workspace geometry and padding.
