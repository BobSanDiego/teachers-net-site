## Reconciliation summary

The package preserves Core Terms, Portable Views, subscriber policies, interests/onboarding, chatboard/path/group mapping, relationship governance, and the Communications Platform lifecycle as distinct but connected workstreams. It accelerates identity, privacy, consent, notification, suppression, and oversight contracts; resequences provider delivery after those contracts; and defers relationships, recommendations, AI, campaigns, analytics, and post-view expansion until their prerequisites are accepted.

The go point is **NO-GO** pending Engineering Director review, unresolved production evidence, external research decisions, and explicit bounded M1 authorization. The exact next decision is whether to approve or revise this package and authorize M1.
